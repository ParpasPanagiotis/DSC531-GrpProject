The dataset consists of 2205 observations (customers) with 27 variables (some of them are just indicators that can be wisely grouped with other variables) related to the marketing data of a company. The company wants to have a full exploratory data analysis in order to better understand the profiles of its customers and the covariation between variables. As already mentioned, there are 27 columns in the dataset. These are:
Income: The customer's annual household income (in dollars).
Kidhome: The number of small children in the customer's household.
Teenhome: The number of teenagers in the customer's household.
Recency: The number of days since the customer's last purchase.
MntWines: The amount spent on wines in the last 2 years.
MntFruits: The amount spent on fruits in the last 2 years.
MntMeatProducts: The amount spent on meat products in the last 2 years.
MntFishProducts: The amount spent on fish products in the last 2 years.
MntSweetProducts: The amount spent on sweet products in the last 2 years.
NumDealsPurchases: The number of purchases made with a discount.
NumWebPurchases: The number of purchases made through the company's website.
NumCatalogPurchases: The number of purchases made using the company's catalogue.
NumStorePurchases: The number of purchases made directly in stores.
NumWebVisitsMonth:  The number of visits on the company's website during the last month.
Complain: Takes the value of 1 if the customer has complained in the last 2 years.
Age: The age of the customer.
Customer_Days: Number of days being a customer.
Divorced: Takes the value of 1 if the customer is divorced.
Married: Takes the value of 1 if the customer is married.
Single: Takes the value of 1 if the customer is single.
Living_together: Takes the value of 1 if the customer lives together with another person but are not married.
Widow: Takes the value of 1 if the customer is a widow.
Up_to_high_school: 1 if the highest level of education of the customer is high school.
Up_to_undergraduate_degree: 1 if the highest level of education of the customer is an undergraduate degree.
Up_to_masters_degree: 1 if the highest level of education of the customer is a master's degree.
PhD: 1 if the customer has a PhD.
Total.amount.spent: The total amount spent on products in the last two years.
